About Me

Hi, I’m Rafael! I’m a computer science student at the University of South Florida who’s always looking to grow, build cool things, and make an impact. I most recently interned at Capital One, where I revamped alerting experiences used by 2M+ customers, and before that at Chime, where I built tools that make life easier for customer support teams.


I started out coding at a small company, MTW Solutions, back in Kansas City. We didn’t use fancy frameworks, just real logic and real problems. That experience taught me how to think through challenges and take ownership .


At USF, I served as Vice President of External Affairs for SHPE, leading a team of 15. We connected students to companies, hosted events with NASA and Amazon, and raised $13.3K to create opportunities for our 1,200+ members.


I’m always learning, whether it’s Spanish, machine learning, AI tools, or stepping out of my comfort zone with boxing, guitar, bachata, and salsa.
